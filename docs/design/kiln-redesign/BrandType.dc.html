Why keep Geist

I looked at replacing it with something warmer to match the clay. I'd keep Geist:

Narrow and calm. Dense tables and long copy stay readable at 13 px on a phone.
A true mono twin. Ids, ₹ and timestamps line up in columns; Geist Mono shares metrics, so mixing in one line doesn't jump.
Personality lives elsewhere. The clay, chalk and cast colours already carry warmth; a characterful UI face would fight them.
Free, on Google Fonts, already loaded on every screen. Check ₹ and dotless ı render in Geist before shipping; fall back to Geist Mono's ₹ if not.

Considered: IBM Plex Sans (wider, busier tables), Söhne (licence cost), Inter (generic).
